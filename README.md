# Thank you, Judge!

A small round robot holds up one giant glowing sunflower while a little
girl hugs it from behind, laughing with her eyes squeezed shut — and a
golden retriever puppy leaps joyfully beside them. Above them hangs a
hand-painted wooden sign that reads "THANK YOU, JUDGE!", with confetti
and soap bubbles drifting through warm golden afternoon light.

The idea is a thank-you in the picture itself: the robot, the child and
the puppy are all delighted at once, the sunflower is one bold shape
that reads instantly even at 300px, and the sign speaks straight to the
judge who is looking at it. Warm gold light pulls the whole image toward
one happy mood.
